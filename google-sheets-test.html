📊 Google Sheets Viewer - Pure JavaScript

No backend needed! Fetches directly from Google Sheets

💡 How it works:
1. Paste your Google Sheets share link
2. Make sure the sheet is set to "Anyone with the link can view"
3. Click "Get Data"
4. The sheet will be downloaded as Excel and displayed!

Format: Works with any Google Sheets URL
https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/SHEET_ID/edit
